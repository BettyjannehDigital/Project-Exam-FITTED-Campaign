# How to Post Your Campaign Content: Full Guide

*Written for the FITTED client so she could post the five campaign pieces herself. This is the final version.*

Everything for all 5 pieces: when to post, and exactly how for the two that need some manual work.

## 1. Questions Story: Monday, 3:00 PM

1. Add a new Instagram Story and upload the graphic as the background.
2. Tap the sticker icon and select **Questions** (not Poll). Questions lets people type their own open answer rather than picking from fixed options.
3. Type the prompt: "What type of workout is your routine missing?"
4. Position the Questions sticker over the matching spot on the graphic, then post.
5. Check responses afterwards. Replies come in as a list you can view and reply to individually, which is also useful engagement data for the campaign.

## 2. "You Asked, We Answered" Announcement: Wednesday, 12:00 PM

No extra steps. Upload the graphic directly as an Instagram Story at 12:00 PM. It is ready to go as-is.

## 3. "Mark Your Calendar" Reminder: Thursday, 6:00 PM

Same as above: post the graphic directly as an Instagram Story at 6:00 PM. No extra steps.

## 4. Main Workout Video: Friday, 3:00 PM

This goes up as a proper Instagram Reel (not just a Story), so it needs a caption and hashtags.

**Caption (draft, personalise as you like):**

> Leg day = deadlift day. Sumo, conventional, RDLs, trap bar: we covered every angle (plus goblet squats + leg curls to finish it off). Built for bodies that move and turn heads. Which deadlift variation is your favourite? Let us know below.
>
> Shop the look → fitted.no | Join the FITTED community

**Recommended hashtags**

Instagram's current guidance favours fewer, more targeted hashtags over long lists. Aim for 5–8, mixing broad, niche and community tags rather than generic ones like #fitness alone.

| Type | Hashtags |
| --- | --- |
| Broad | #WorkoutMotivation #FitnessMotivation |
| Niche (leg day specific) | #LegDay #DeadliftDay #RDL #GobletSquat |
| Community | #GymGirl #GirlsWhoLift #Fitfam |
| Brand / positioning | #FittedActivewear #CurvyFitness #BodyPositiveFitness |
| Local reach (Norway) | #TreningNorge #TreningOslo |

Pick 6–8 in total rather than every tag above, e.g. 1 broad + 2 niche + 1 community + 1–2 brand + 1 local.

## 5. Recap / "Close the Loop" Story: Sunday, 7:00 PM

The video is already posted as a Reel by this point, so you re-share it inside a Story and add the recap graphic on top. Do it in this order:

1. Go to your own profile and find Friday's Leg-Day Reel.
2. Tap the Share icon underneath it (paper airplane) and select **Add post to your story**. This opens a new Story with the video as a small, movable clip.
3. Tap the sticker icon, select **Photo**, and choose the recap graphic ("Missed Friday's Leg-Day?") from your gallery. This adds the graphic as its own layer.
4. Press and hold the video clip and, while still holding, slowly drag your finger down so the video appears on top of the recap graphic. Keep adjusting (drag and pinch to resize) until it fits inside the marked box on the graphic.
5. Once it is positioned nicely, post it.
